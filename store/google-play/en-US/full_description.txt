Rádio Animu is an anime radio from Brazil playing anime music live, 24 hours a day. Anime openings and endings, anisongs, J-pop, vocaloid and rhythm game music, with live DJs and an otaku community made by fans.

LISTEN TO LIVE ANIME RADIO
• Online anime radio in 64, 192 or 320 kbps
• Cover art, anime, artist and the song playing now, synced with what you hear
• Show schedule with the live DJ and the time left on each song
• Keeps playing in the background and reconnects on its own if the connection drops
• Controls in the notification and on the lock screen
• Live audio visualizer on the home screen

SYNCED LYRICS FOR ANIME SONGS
• Karaoke-style lyrics that follow the song line by line
• For Japanese songs: original script, romaji and word-by-word readings

REQUEST YOUR FAVORITE ANIME SONGS
• Search the library and request the anime opening or ending you want to hear
• Follow your request's status and send messages to the DJ during live shows
• See the latest songs requested and played on the radio

YOUR STATS
• Listening time, songs heard, day streak and heatmap
• Create a card to share with your friends

YOUR ACCOUNT
• Sign in with Discord, Google, Fluxer or Apple, or with an email code if you already have an Animu Connect account
• Listening doesn't require an account: signing in is only needed for requests and your profile
• Delete your account at any time inside the app

MADE YOUR WAY
• Available in Portuguese, English, Spanish and Japanese
• Cover art saved on your device, with storage controls and data saver mode
• Voice command to start playing and haptic feedback on buttons

Rádio Animu is a non-profit project made out of love for anime and otaku culture. No ads, no in-app purchases and no tracking. The app's source code is open and available on GitHub.
